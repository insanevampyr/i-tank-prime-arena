# ITank Prime Arena Rules Ledger

## Purpose

This ledger distinguishes verified Prime rules, approved designer corrections, locked Arena adaptations, provisional values, and unresolved items. It prevents implementation details from silently becoming game rules.

No missing formula, threshold, probability, creature statistic, Special effect, or exception may be inferred from genre convention. A disputed or incomplete rule remains unimplemented until its authoritative source is recorded and a designer ruling is obtained when necessary.

## Authority order

1. Actual ITank Prime V3 text and applicable Special descriptions.
2. Explicit designer corrections.
3. Approved Arena adaptations in the Combat Specification.
4. Provisional tuning values identified as provisional.

Legacy creature records and older Special sheets remain historical references. They do not automatically override a higher-authority source.

## Phase 1 rulings and remaining gates

The Phase 1 Task 2 designer rulings resolve defense sequencing/cost/awareness, shield destruction, armor coverage, combined-type protection, damage order, natural critical triggers and weights, generic knockback consequences, and canonical movement speed. These are Arena rulings where Prime is silent or requires deterministic automation; they do not rewrite the cited original Prime text.

Running's generic calculation and Arena timing/recovery behavior are resolved by PRIME-V3 and the v1.2/v1.4 addenda. Combat Specification v1.7 and the locked tables approve all ten general critical tables. Remaining critical gates are exact Backfire redirection facts and distinct production Dwarvestek/Grentek firearm/explosive mechanics; unavailable components record a blocked or partial result rather than substitute rewards. Source-specific weakness stages, immunity/weakness/minimum-one imports, and the generic knockback Balance target/modifier also remain data gates. Automatic Dodge selection is resolved for environments that supply complete authoritative candidates; missing collision, terrain, occupancy, hazard, escape, duck or path-cost data remains an explicit pending boundary. Special-specific timing, NAC chains, Special Resistance, magical damage, blood-loss subsystems and source-specific knockback remain deferred scope.

Task 16 supplies complete authoritative collision, occupancy, path and known-hazard facts for its single test arena. Task 17 supersedes its two deferred boundaries: attacks revalidate range and line of sight at final effect before ACC, and prone actors may crawl at 25% effective walking MR without Running. Prime V3 page 36 does not provide numeric prone penalties, while pages 95-98 provide a size/location ACC chart with no prone posture entry. Accordingly, the current same-size torso fixture uses the chart's `No Change` result and no blanket prone ACC modifier; other size/location rows must be transcribed from the chart before use.

Task 9 makes duck permission an attack/effect data fact. `allowed` admits an otherwise legal in-place candidate, `prohibited` rejects it, and `unresolved` skips it while continuing independently legal displacement. If unresolved ducking is the only viable candidate, the successful Dodge remains pending. Prime page 20 supports geometry-specific permission but does not justify a blanket policy for attacks or Specials.

The v1.3/v1.5 action-timing amendment resolves the former initial-Act ambiguity: no Act bank or starting reserve exists. Integer scheduling rounds each cumulative rational boundary upward to the first whole microsecond, preserving exact five-second totals for fractional AT sequences such as AT 3 without cumulative drift.

Task 6 resolves generic opening order and physical ACC without inventing weapon content. Prime does not assign real-time seconds between opening positions. Arena therefore gates first declarations in verified order at the current simulation timestamp: equal total uses higher base INIT, and an exact remaining tie shares one eligible rank. Production weapon ranges, aimed-location matrices, actor/equipment data and source-specific ACC exceptions remain source-data gates.
