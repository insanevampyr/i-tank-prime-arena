# ITank Prime Arena Rules Ledger

## Purpose

This ledger distinguishes verified Prime rules, approved designer corrections, locked Arena adaptations, provisional values, and unresolved items. It prevents implementation details from silently becoming game rules.

No missing formula, threshold, probability, creature statistic, Special effect, or exception may be inferred from genre convention. A disputed or incomplete rule remains unimplemented until its authoritative source is recorded and a designer ruling is obtained when necessary.

## Authority order

1. Actual ITank Prime V3 text and applicable Special descriptions.
2. Explicit designer corrections.
3. Approved Arena adaptations in the Combat Specification.
4. Provisional tuning values identified as provisional.

Legacy creature records and older Special sheets remain historical references. They do not automatically override a higher-authority source.

## Phase 1 rulings and remaining gates

The Phase 1 Task 2 designer rulings resolve defense sequencing/cost/awareness, shield destruction, armor coverage, combined-type protection, damage order, natural critical triggers and weights, generic knockback consequences, and canonical movement speed. These are Arena rulings where Prime is silent or requires deterministic automation; they do not rewrite the cited original Prime text.

Running's generic calculation and Arena timing/recovery behavior are now resolved by PRIME-V3 and the v1.2/v1.4 addenda. Remaining physical-combat gates are fractional multiplier rounding, authored critical table entries, expanded-weapon critical effects, and the generic knockback Balance target/modifier. Automatic Dodge selection is resolved for environments that supply complete authoritative candidates; missing collision, terrain, occupancy, hazard, escape, duck or path-cost data remains an explicit pending boundary. Special-specific timing, NAC chains, Special Resistance, magical damage, Dwarvestek/Grentek, blood-loss subsystems and source-specific knockback remain deferred scope.

Task 9 makes duck permission an attack/effect data fact. `allowed` admits an otherwise legal in-place candidate, `prohibited` rejects it, and `unresolved` skips it while continuing independently legal displacement. If unresolved ducking is the only viable candidate, the successful Dodge remains pending. Prime page 20 supports geometry-specific permission but does not justify a blanket policy for attacks or Specials.

The v1.3/v1.5 action-timing amendment resolves the former initial-Act ambiguity: no Act bank or starting reserve exists. Integer scheduling rounds each cumulative rational boundary upward to the first whole microsecond, preserving exact five-second totals for fractional AT sequences such as AT 3 without cumulative drift.

Task 6 resolves generic opening order and physical ACC without inventing weapon content. Prime does not assign real-time seconds between opening positions. Arena therefore gates first declarations in verified order at the current simulation timestamp: equal total uses higher base INIT, and an exact remaining tie shares one eligible rank. Production weapon ranges, aimed-location matrices, actor/equipment data and source-specific ACC exceptions remain source-data gates.

## Task 1 implementation boundary

Task 1 implements only neutral infrastructure: integer timestamps, five-second boundary calculation, seeded random generation, deterministic event identifiers, dependency and resource-commitment fields, and queue ordering. It does not interpret payloads or implement attacks, defenses, Specials, NAC resolution, interruptions, damage, character progression, loot, creatures, AI, saves, or networking.

## Task 2 implementation boundary

Task 2 wraps authoritative simulation output in a sequence-numbered, transport-neutral protocol and verifies that two passive observers converge after duplicate and out-of-order delivery. State digests cover the canonical observed event transcript. This is not network transport, rollback, prediction, matchmaking, account state, or gameplay resolution.

It does not add attacks, damage, critical-effect tables, knockback resolution, Specials, NACs, enemy AI or networking.

## Phase 1 Task 4 implementation boundary

It does not implement attacks, damage, defenses, resource spending, Specials, NAC mechanics, AI, loot, progression or transport networking.

## Phase 1 Task 5 implementation boundary

Phase 1 Task 5 removes Task 4's superseded stored-Act fields and gates. Every action now begins with zero completed intervals and schedules cumulative `5 seconds / AT` boundaries from its start or immediately continuous predecessor. Idle gaps reset the timing anchor; sequential actions preserve rational phase so fractional AT values do not drift. Dependencies, interruptions, movement, authoritative snapshots and simulated-client synchronization remain intact. NAC and Special mechanics remain unimplemented.

## Phase 1 Task 6 implementation boundary

Phase 1 Task 6 adds deterministic opening `1d10 + INIT`, first-declaration ordering, exact-tie simultaneous eligibility, source-referenced physical attack definitions, fail-closed target/equipment/range/location/line-of-sight checks, final-effect ACC rolls, RB-aware adjusted rolls, natural critical triggers and `pending_defense` or `missed` results. Successful ACC never bypasses defense and never changes HPC. All included weapons, ranges and modifiers are labeled engine test fixtures rather than production Prime data. Defense rolls, damage, armor, actor-specific content, critical consequences, Specials and NAC effects remain unimplemented.

## Phase 1 Task 7 implementation boundary

Phase 1 Task 7 extends `pending_defense` with authority-owned, configurable Dodge, weapon Block, shield Block and Physical Soak candidates. Every candidate records eligibility, equipment, target/modifier, natural and adjusted d20, RNG draw index, critical trigger, result, resource spending and causal event identifiers. Failed legal defenses fall through once; weapon and shield Blocks remain independent; attempting Dodge or Physical Soak excludes the other; surprise/unawareness rejects all active physical defenses. Successful selected Dodge displacement spends exact round MR, while ambiguous destination selection remains synchronized and pending without movement or spending. Successful defense becomes `defended`; exhausted defenses become `defenses_exhausted`. Neither state rolls damage, changes HPC, reduces shield SHP or applies critical consequences. All actor/item values remain labeled test fixtures rather than production Prime content.

## Phase 1 Task 8 implementation boundary

Phase 1 Task 8 adds authority-owned automatic Dodge selection over an explicit environmental candidate set. Complete candidate data records path clearance, collision clearance, legal terrain, occupancy, attack escape or legal duck sufficiency, integer path cost and a source-owned nonnegative hazard tier. The selector rejects illegal or over-half-MR candidates, chooses the nearest candidate in the lowest known hazard tier, and resolves exact ties by stable candidate ID then coordinates. A legal in-place duck is the zero-cost nearest candidate. Incomplete environmental data keeps a successful Dodge in `pending_dodge_displacement`; a complete set with no legal candidate skips Dodge without a roll, so Physical Soak remains legal until either Dodge or Soak is actually attempted. Selection evidence is serialized in the defense attempt and authoritative snapshot. No map geometry, hazard immunity, damage, critical consequence, SHP mutation or HPC mutation is introduced.

## Phase 1 Task 9 implementation boundary

Phase 1 Task 9 adds three-state duck eligibility and the authoritative physical-damage transaction. Only `defenses_exhausted` attacks may roll source-declared weapon dice, add an explicitly eligible SB, apply approved integer multipliers, select struck-location armor, use the lowest TAL for combined B/S/P types, subtract NAL and change HPC once. Exact-type immunity/weakness data requires an explicit stage. Missing dice policy, SB eligibility, TAL, NAL, location multiplier, minimum-one policy, critical effect, rounding or stacking data fails closed before unsupported resolution. Successful defenses remain non-damaging. A successful shield Block now spends exactly one SHP; 0 SHP makes it unusable and removes its equipped reference. Production item data, fractional rounding, critical consequences, Special damage, repair and inventory integration remain deferred.
